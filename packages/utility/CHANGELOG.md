### Features

- **Utility builder**: `createUtility()` with `add`, `use`, `useAll`, `build` and
  `resolve`.
- **Plugins**: typed additions, `requires` ordering, duplicate detection and explicit
  `override`.
- **Descriptors**: `UtilityPair`, `UtilityMap`, `ResolveUtilities`, `PluginUnion`,
  and `same()` for mode-independent implementations.
